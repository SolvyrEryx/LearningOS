# Syllabus map: _template

The syllabus — not the textbook's contents page — defines what counts as covered. Fill one section
per unit, citing the syllabus document as `[source_id p.N]` so the mapping is traceable.

Statuses below are a human summary. The authoritative per-concept state is `concept_mastery.json`
in this subject's directory under `03_MEMORY/subjects/`; regenerate the summary rather than
maintaining it by hand.

## How to fill this in

For each unit: the official wording, its exam weighting if known, the concept ids that cover it,
the sources that teach it, and anything the syllabus demands that your material does not cover.
That last line is the useful one — uncovered syllabus points are where exam surprises come from.
