# Learner profile

Durable facts about how this learner learns. Cross-subject, slow-changing, and read at the start of
every session. One-off session details do not belong here — they belong in the event log.

Only record what the learner has actually told you or what the evidence clearly shows across several
sessions. No inferred personality, no speculation, no personal information they did not volunteer
for this purpose.
