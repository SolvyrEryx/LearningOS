# Open questions: _template

Questions raised during sessions that are not yet answered. Parking a question here is better than
answering it badly, and better than losing it.

Each entry gets a date, who raised it, the concept it touches, and its status. Answered entries stay
in the file, moved to the Answered section — the history of what confused you is useful data.

## Status values

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `open` | not yet answered |
| `blocked` | needs something external: a lecturer, a source you do not have, an OCR pass |
| `answered` | resolved; moved to the Answered section with the answer |
| `dropped` | deliberately abandoned, with the reason |
