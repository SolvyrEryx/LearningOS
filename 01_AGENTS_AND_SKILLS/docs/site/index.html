A few practical things

Before you begin.

What do I need installed?+

Python 3.11 or newer, and an AI agent that can read and write local files and run shell commands. The core Python toolkit uses the standard library. Your chosen agent may need its own account or subscription.

Can I start without a syllabus?+

Yes. Pick a subject or skill and one thing you want to be able to do. Exams, deadlines and uploaded notes are optional. When you add sources, your tutor can teach from them and cite the page or line.

What stays on my computer?+

Your workspace holds your documents, attempts, learning state and handovers. The study toolkit makes no network calls. If your AI tutor uses a hosted model, content it reads into the conversation is processed by that provider. Local files do not make the model’s inference local.

Where do lessons and progress live?+

Lessons happen in your AI agent’s chat. Your tutor saves evidence into the workspace and generates the Learning Atlas, an offline report you open in a browser. Ask /atlas to rebuild it, then reload the report. Ask /save before closing your chat.
