The learning method

Knowledge you can
reconstruct and use.

A polymath’s education needs breadth and a way to go deep. Follow a question into a new field, find the ideas it depends on, and work towards an explanation you can defend.

LearningOS gives your tutor a method for that work: diagnose, explain, reduce support, assess and revisit. What you can do unaided determines the next lesson.

Use it for a theorem, a code interview, a historical argument or an exam syllabus. Each subject keeps its own evidence, misconceptions and path forward.

Read the methodology ↗
  1. 01

    Prerequisite graphs & first-principles study

    Build a concept catalogue and dependency graph from your syllabus or chosen goal. Your tutor probes the foundations, moves from concrete examples to formal reasoning, and fades worked examples as you become independent.

  2. 02

    Assessment beyond a correct answer

    Examine explanation, application, recall, derivation, assumptions, transfer, method selection and execution. Your tutor diagnoses the first broken step, records hints, and tests a misconception again on a different task. Mastery requires repeated unaided evidence, including delayed retrieval.

  3. 03

    Spaced retrieval & unfamiliar problems

    Revisit concepts on a 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35-day ladder. Interleave topics so you practise selecting a method as well as executing it. Review priorities account for overdue retrieval, prerequisite gaps and recurring mistakes.

  4. 04

    Source-grounded reasoning

    Index your notes, syllabi and textbooks. Trace claims to a page or line, preserve original documents, and separate source-backed explanations from general knowledge. Keep uncertain or disputed claims visible as open questions.

  5. 05

    A Learning Atlas of your understanding ↗

    Ten views connect your concept map, learning evidence, revision, exam results, error patterns and memory. Inspect the responses and judgments behind a concept’s status, then follow its sources and next actions.

  6. 06

    Timed practice & targeted retesting

    Prepare verified question banks, run timed mock or sectional practice, inspect error patterns and revisit unseen variants of failed questions. Reserve holdout questions for assessment. Historical attempts retain their question versions, while exam scores remain separate from concept mastery.

Inside the Learning Atlas

Inspect your learning
across ten views.

Open a self-contained report in your browser and move from an overview of your studies to the exact response behind a learning judgment.

The concept map, charts, search, fonts and included evidence travel with the generated file. The Atlas works offline. Your tutor records the work and rebuilds the report; reload it after a save.

Search the memory included in your Atlas, then open the matching record.

  1. 01 / 02

    Overview & Learning map

    See subjects, concept-state distributions, reviews due, recent activity and a study plan for your time budget. Filter the map by subject or learning state, inspect prerequisite links, and use the complete concept table to find the next foundation to assess.

  2. 03 / 04

    Learning evidence & Revision

    Read actual questions, responses, feedback, hints, annotated solution steps and judgments across eight dimensions. Keep saved explanations and uncertainties alongside them. The revision queue brings together due dates, learning states and the exact first activity for each concept.

  3. 05 / 06

    Exam performance & Error patterns

    Review marks, question-level answers and solutions, timing coverage, pauses and prior exposure. Inspect confidence calibration when recorded, then connect lost marks and recurring mistakes to specific repair tasks. Open-test answers and solutions stay hidden in the Atlas until completion.

  4. 07 / 08

    Progress & Memory explorer

    Separate unaided, delayed and novel successes. Read a 12-week assessed-attempt heatmap, daily evidence tables and test-score trends. Search events, notes, handovers and checkpoints by keyword, subject, record type or date, then inspect the matching record.

  5. 09 / 10

    Source library & Data health

    Find source identities, document types, page counts, local paths and extraction warnings. Inspect validation errors, remedies, unavailable files and SHA-256 input fingerprints. A coverage manifest shows what the snapshot includes and what stays outside it.

Working with the system

Build an education
you can inspect.

Can I pursue several fields?+

Keep mathematics, philosophy, programming or any other subject in the same personal workspace. Each has its own concept catalogue, sources, misconceptions and mastery evidence. Your tutor saves the current subject before switching. A goal, question or skill is enough to begin; syllabi and exam dates are optional.

How does the tutor adapt to me?+

Your record includes recurring mistakes, unresolved misconceptions, hint usage and approaches that helped or failed. The tutor reads that history to choose its explanation, check prerequisites and adjust support. You can inspect the evidence and the next action rather than relying on an unexplained score.

What earns a claim of mastery?+

A checked learning cycle needs both explanation and application without hints or solution exposure to count as independent success. Provisional mastery requires repeated independent success, a non-familiar task, a delayed check and no unresolved linked misconception in the qualifying attempts. The mastery estimate is a transparent heuristic; its evidence and rules are inspectable.

What makes the memory durable?+

Learning evidence lives in an append-only event history, with validated snapshots, checkpoints and an exact handover. Backups carry file hashes, and interrupted saves can be completed from matching records. Your tutor must record the work: say /save before stopping, /continue when returning, and /atlas to rebuild the offline report.

What do I need to begin?+

Python 3.11 or newer, and an AI agent that can read and write local files and run shell commands. The core toolkit uses the standard library; PDF text extraction optionally uses pypdf. Your chosen agent may need its own account or subscription. Teaching and grading depend on that model and the sources you check against.

What stays on my computer?+

Your workspace holds your documents, attempts, learning state and handovers. The study toolkit makes no network calls. If your AI tutor uses a hosted model, content it reads into the conversation is processed by that provider. Local files do not make the model’s inference local.
